**Feedback and Fixes**
After having a conversation and asking for feedback with some of my friends in class. I notice that my website is more about this class rather than my own portfolio. I have to change some of the text to make this more personal. The overall design received very positive feedback from many for the aesthetic. There were no complaints about the navigation. Although, one of them mentioned that the background could be a little distracting. I personally don't want to remove it because it's my style preference to include photography into my design. However, I did lower the saturation and brightness a little bit to give more contrast between the subject and the background. Another friend of mine recommended me to add a few more animations. I had a try and I found that animating can be incredibly time consuming in Figma because I have to individually animate each elements, eventhough I wanted the same hover animation in most of them. I ended up just making the background stick to the top when scrolling so that there is a little bit of a parallax effect between the subject and background. It can also seperate the two even further. Other than that is just a feedback about some of my spellings.

**Convert to mobile phone format**
After everything seems to be done. I then convert it into a mobile phone format. I used iPhone 16 Pro Max for the frame. I reduce the size of the images and the title to make it fit in the format. However, I tried keeping the body paragraph the same size as it is harder to read a long text on a phone. I put the body paragraph at the bottom of all the images, seperating in half so it's easier to differentiate between the images and the body on moblie phone. Lastly I switched 'Extra Light' fonts to either 'Light' or 'Regular' font because it might be harder to read 'Extra Light'on a mobile phone.
